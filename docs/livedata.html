Routing ignores the roster notes

Only 14 of 80 asks land in a stated focus area, and those convert at 64% vs 32%.Focus area is the single strongest predictor of an intro in this data, and it is almost never used when choosing who to ask.
The notes predicted the failures.Owen Trask ("tapped no more than twice a month") was asked 3 times in 12 months and sent zero intros; Dana Whitfield ("travels constantly; slow to respond") got 4 asks and booked no meetings.

Where the notes were right

Elena Duvall, "deep but narrow".6 of her 17 asks were heavy industry, and that is where her intros came from.
Marcus Aldridge, "asked far more than anyone else", capacity 4/month.15 asks with the weakest response rate of the four heavily-used connectors (53%).
Tomás Beckett, "fast responder, broad but shallow".70% response rate but only 50% of those responses became an intro.
